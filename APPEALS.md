# Appeals

If Asli lists something of yours, or misses something it should have caught, this
is how to reach a human.

## Ask for a review

Open an issue using the appeal form, or email the address in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if the matter is sensitive. Include:

- the exact domain, number or sender name
- your relationship to it
- anything that shows the listing is wrong, such as when you took the domain over

You do not need an account, a lawyer, or a particular tone. A short message with
the facts is enough.

## What happens next

1. The listing is read again against the evidence stored with it.
2. If the evidence no longer supports it, the entry is removed within 72 hours of
   the appeal being seen, and the removal is recorded with its reason.
3. If the evidence still supports it, you get a written answer saying why, with
   the evidence quoted, and you can reply with more.
4. If the case is genuinely unclear, the listing is suspended while it is worked
   out. Doubt goes in favour of removal.

## Escalation

If you disagree with the answer, say so in the same issue and ask for a second
reviewer. A different person reads the case from the start. Their decision and
their reasoning are published.

## Timing

Target for a first response: 72 hours. Nothing is guaranteed, because this is
volunteer work, but a missed target is reported in the monthly transparency
report rather than hidden.
